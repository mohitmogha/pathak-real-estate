## 1. Executive Summary & Google Local Algorithm

Google decides which businesses appear in the **Local 3-Pack** (the top 3 map results displayed on Search & Maps) based on three fundamental mathematical signals:

1. **Relevance (Weight: ~32%)**:
   - Matches the searcher's query to your Categories, Services, Description, Attributes, and Website content.
   - Target keywords: *Real estate agent in Uttam Nagar*, *Property dealer in Jeevan Park*, *Flats for sale in Janakpuri*, *Commercial shops in West Delhi*, *Plots for sale in Uttam Nagar*.
2. **Prominence (Weight: ~38%)**:
   - Online trust, review volume, review velocity (consistent new reviews), 4.8+ star rating, owner response rate, high-authority local citations (99acres, Magicbricks, Justdial), and backlinks.
3. **Distance & Proximity (Weight: ~30%)**:
   - The user's location relative to your verified business address and defined service area radius.
