# Review Generation & Reputation Acceleration Playbook

Reviews are the **#1 local ranking signal** for prominence. Google's algorithm evaluates:
1. **Review Count**: Aim for 30+ reviews to beat local competitors in Uttam Nagar & Janak Puri.
2. **Review Velocity**: 2–4 genuine reviews every week is significantly better than 30 reviews added in a single day.
3. **Keyword Inclusion in Reviews**: When reviews mention words like *"2BHK flat in Uttam Nagar"*, *"plot in Jeevan Park"*, or *"Pathak Real Estate"*, Google ranks you higher for those exact search queries.
4. **Owner Response Rate**: Google rewards profiles that reply to 100% of their reviews with higher visibility.

---

## 1. How to Get Your Direct Google Review Link

1. Go to [Google Business Profile Manager](https://business.google.com/) or search `my business` on Google while logged into your business email.
2. Under your business dashboard, click **"Ask for reviews"** (or **"Get more reviews"**).
3. Copy your unique short link (it looks like `https://g.page/r/.../review` or `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=...`).
4. Replace `[YOUR_DIRECT_REVIEW_LINK]` in the templates below with this link.

## 4. Crucial Do's and Don'ts for Google Reviews

* **DO NOT** ask clients to connect to your office Wi-Fi while submitting a review. Google checks IP addresses; multiple reviews originating from the exact same Wi-Fi IP address can be flagged as fraudulent and removed.
* **DO** ask clients to post reviews from their personal mobile internet (4G/5G) or from their home/workplace.
* **DO NOT** incentivize reviews with cash, gifts, or discounts (violates Google anti-incentive policy).
* **DO** aim for steady acquisition (e.g., 2–4 reviews per week) rather than 50 reviews in one afternoon.
